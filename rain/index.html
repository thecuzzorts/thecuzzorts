About these totals

Totals are NOAA precipitation estimates on about a 4 km grid. A pin reads the cell around that point. The 24-hour column is the latest hourly product. The longer columns are daily accumulations that end at 12 UTC, which is 7 a.m. CDT or 6 a.m. CST in central Illinois. Thirty, 180, and 365 days are day counts. The time under a location is when this browser received the value.
