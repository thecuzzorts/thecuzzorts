About this page

Positions come from volunteer ADS-B receivers through the free adsb.fi and adsb.lol feeds. A GitHub Action reads them every 30 seconds while a plane is flying and every few minutes otherwise, so the map runs a minute or more behind. Between updates the marker is moved forward along its track and speed.

Takeoff and landing airports are the nearest jet-capable field to where the plane was first and last seen. Times marked ~ are estimates. Flights that fly out of receiver coverage show where the signal was lost.
